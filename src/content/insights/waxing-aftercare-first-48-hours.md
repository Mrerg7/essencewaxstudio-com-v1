The appointment is half the result. The first 48 hours decide whether skin recovers clean or spends a week irritated.

## First 24 hours

- **No heat.** Hot tubs, saunas, steam rooms, and very hot showers open follicles before they have closed. Lukewarm is fine.
- **No sweat.** Gym, spin, and long runs wait a day. Sweat and friction on open follicles are the two most common causes of post-wax bumps.
- **No pools, oceans, or hot tubs.** Chlorine and bacteria both have a straight line into freshly opened skin. Wait 48 hours minimum for open water.
- **No tight clothing** over the area. Waistbands and seams rub; loose cotton until the skin settles.
- **No fragrance, no deodorant actives** on the waxed area. Unscented moisturizer only.

## Days two and three

Once the first day has passed, the priority flips from protection to **prevention of ingrowns**:

1. **Start gentle exfoliation on day three** — not day one. A soft scrub or washcloth in the shower, two or three times a week from here on.
2. **Keep the area moisturized.** Dry skin traps the growing hair under the surface.
3. **Do not pick.** Touching, digging, or squeezing turns a normal follicle into an angry one.

## When something looks wrong

A little redness and warmth in the first hours is normal. Spreading rash, swelling, or pustules that last past 48 hours are not — contact the studio, and see a clinician if it spreads.

Consistent sessions matter too: regrowth on the 3 to 4 week cycle gets finer over time, and exfoliation between visits is what keeps each cycle clean.
