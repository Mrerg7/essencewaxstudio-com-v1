Preparation decides how the appointment feels more than almost anything the aesthetician does in the room. This is the 72-hour checklist.

## 72 hours before

- **Grow to ¼–½ inch.** About two to three weeks of growth for most people. If you are unsure, go slightly longer — the wax can trim down, but it cannot grip what is not there.
- **Skip retinoids and acids.** Retinol, tretinoin, AHAs, BHAs, and any exfoliating acid thin the top layer of skin and make it bruise more easily. Stop 48–72 hours ahead.
- **No self-tanner, no fresh tan.** Fresh pigment and wax do not mix; surface color lifts with the wax.
- **Book away from your period if you are sensitive.** The days just before and during are typically the most tender.

## 24 hours before

- **Exfoliate — the day before, not the day of.** A gentle scrub or dry brush the evening prior lifts dead skin so the wax can grip hair cleanly. Exfoliating morning-of leaves skin reactive.
- **Moisturize that night, then stop.** Lotion, oils, and deodorant on the day of the appointment reduce grip.
- **Wear loose clothing.** Cotton or relaxed fit. Tight waistbands rub freshly opened follicles afterward.

## Day of

- **Clean skin, no product.** Shower beforehand; arrive without lotion, oil, or perfume on the area.
- **Eat something.** First-timers sometimes feel lightheaded on an empty stomach.
- **Expect short, sharp pulls, not sustained burning.** The sensation of hard wax is brief by design. Say so if anything feels wrong — a good studio adjusts temperature or technique rather than pushing through.
